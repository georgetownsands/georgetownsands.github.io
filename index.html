Oceanside Living in the Heart of Duck, NC

Georgetown Sands is an oceanside townhome community nestled on the northern Outer Banks. Just steps over the dunes to the Atlantic Ocean beach, and a leisurely stroll along the Duck Trail to soundfront boardwalks, world-class dining, and boutique shops.
